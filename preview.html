QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about dental treatments, timings, and comfort at Nirmala Dental.

Is Root Canal Treatment (RCT) painful at Nirmala Dental?

Not at all! With modern computer-controlled rotary endodontics and targeted digital local anesthesia administered by Dr. Amit Kumar, root canal therapy at our clinic is virtually painless and usually completed in a single comfortable sitting.

How long do dental implants last?

Dental implants are made of biocompatible medical-grade titanium that fuses directly with your jawbone. With regular daily brushing, flossing, and periodic check-ups, dental implants can last a lifetime.

Are clear aligners as effective as traditional metal braces?

Yes! Invisible clear aligners provide the exact same orthodontic results as metal braces for mild to complex teeth misalignment, but with the added benefits of being 100% invisible, removable during meals, and wire-free.
